### ⚡ About Me

I am **Farhan Hibatullah**, a Full-Stack Developer & UI/UX Designer at **RS Ananda Bekasi**, 3D Creator, and Automation Builder.

My work sits at the intersection of **bulletproof backend architecture and pixel-precise, motion-aware user experience**. I engineer digital products ranging from critical healthcare systems handling real patient appointments to intelligent mobile tracking engines and interactive 3D virtual worlds.

#### 02. Gym Tracker & Nutrition AI Coach
> **Mobile Workout Engine & Intelligent Scientific Progression System**

<table width="100%">
<tr>
<td width="65%" valign="top">

A high-performance mobile application tailored for strength athletes and fitness enthusiasts, combining zero-latency offline tracking with intelligent coaching logic.

- **Local Scientific Rules Engine**: Computes 1RM estimations, effective volume load, and progressive overload recommendations directly on-device.
- **Offline-First Persistence**: Powered by SQLite for zero-latency gym session logging, even in basement gyms without cellular signal.
- **AI Nutrition Cascade**: Dynamic caloric and macronutrient adjustments tailored to body composition shifts and weekly training volume.
- **Tactile UX**: Integrated native haptic feedback (`expo-haptics`) and keep-awake display modes during active training blocks.
